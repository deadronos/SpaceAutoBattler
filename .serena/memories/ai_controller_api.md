AIController (src/core/aiController.ts) — summary and contract
- Purpose: Coordinate and update ship AI decisions (targeting, movement, behavior selection) for all ships in a GameState.
- Primary API:
  - constructor(state: GameState)
  - updateAllShips(dt: number): updates internal AI state and issues movement/targeting decisions per ship
  - optionally exposes utility functions such as chooseTarget, updateShipState
- Inputs: GameState (mutated), dt (seconds)
- Outputs: None (mutates ship.aiState, ship.targetId, and may modify velocities/orientations indirectly via AI decisions). It does not apply physics transforms — physicsStepper or simulateStep handles those.
- Side effects: May create or update per-ship `aiState` fields used by AI logic.
- Contract: Safe to call each simulation tick with a fixed dt. AIController is created lazily and cached on GameState as `state.aiController`.
- Notes: For deterministic behavior ensure RNG usage goes through `state.rng`. Avoid non-deterministic Date usage inside AI logic.
